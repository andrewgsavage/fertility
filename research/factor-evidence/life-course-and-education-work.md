## Cells where evidence suggests changing the tick

1. **Age at first birth × PPR 1→2+** (✓ → doubtful): Skirbekk 2004 find exogenous first-birth delay leaves completed fertility unchanged; the effect is on timing.
2. **Education × Coupling** (✓ → timing only): same natural experiment shows delayed marriage but no change in marrying by 45.
3. **Long working hours × First-birth cond. ASFR** (blank → maybe ✓; PPR 1→2+ ✓ → doubtful): Lambert 2023 finds the effect mainly on first births, but it measures nonstandard schedules, not hours.
4. **Job insecurity × Young first births** (✓ → qualify): Miettinen and Engdahl find no delay, or earlier family formation, among low-educated groups.
5. **Left-behind young men × Young first births** (✓ → qualify): Miettinen finds no negative unemployment effect for young low-educated men.
6. **Partnership stability × PPR 1→2+ / Second+ cond. ASFR** (✓ → qualify): Thomson 2012 finds repartnering offsets most of the loss.
