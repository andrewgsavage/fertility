- **Several verdicts in the source files overstate their findings:**
  - costs: Comolli 2017 / Matysiak 2020 marked "supports" for First-birth cond. ASFR, though the file's own row 57 says no parity result was read; Gözgör 2021 "supports" Second+ on aggregate TFR.
  - costs: Hansen 2017 "contradicts" Income × Second+, but it is aggregate US 1850–2000 with no parity split; at most "no evidence".
  - costs: tick-change item 2 says "Washbrook, Buh and Affuso find positive effects for owners". The recorded Buh finding is about housing expenditure (Home size row) and Affuso is US aggregate with no tenure split.
  - costs: Rents × Second+ row credits Washbrook with "delayed negative responses" for renters; the Washbrook row records only "no positive response".
  - life-course: Long hours × PPR 1→2+ "contradicts": Lambert says the effect is mainly on first births, not absent on second, and it is period evidence. Should be "mixed".
  - life-course: Motherhood penalty × First-birth "mixed" for Bíró 2026, which has no fertility outcome. Should be "no evidence".
  - policy: Childcare × PPR 1→2+ "contradicts" uses a period second-birth transition (Schuß & Azaouagh), which cannot contradict a PPR.
  - social: Paton 2020 (teen fertility) is filed under First-birth cond. ASFR rather than Young first births; Girma 2015 is LARC promotion, closer to Contraception.
  - social: Contraception item 6 says "all ticked columns" are heterogeneous, but only Beauchamp is cited and PPR 0→1 had no evidence.
  - social: Dating apps "contradicts", though the row itself says Rosenfeld is conditional on coupling.
