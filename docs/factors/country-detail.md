One country at a time from the [country comparison](country-comparison.md). The charts show the [outcome grid](outcome-grid.md) outcomes for the chosen country (blue) against the other countries with data (grey), with 2010–2019 shaded. The table ranks the factors by how far they moved towards fewer births in that country.

The child:coupling ratio is the share of women with a child divided by the share in a couple. Where coupling comes in broad age bands, the child share is taken at the band's midpoint (by 30 for couples aged 25–34). Denmark, Finland and the Netherlands have coupling by single year of age, so their ratios also use the same ages on both sides.
