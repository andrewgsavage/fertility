:::{tab-item} Notes
```{note}
Low, middle and high are lower secondary or less (ISCED 0–2), upper secondary (ISCED 3–4) and tertiary (ISCED 5–8) for the European countries; for Mexico, Brazil, Colombia and Taiwan they are defined below. The US has four groups; its age curves are charted separately, and its by-year rates average single years of age within each band. The by-year chart stops at 35–39; Norway's 30–39 band is drawn in the 30–34 panel. Change is relative to the same country's mean at that age in its first three years; ages where that mean is under 5 per 1,000 are left out.
```

```{note}
United States: US Census Bureau [American Community Survey 1-year PUMS](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/microdata.html), 2008, 2010, 2012, 2015, 2017, 2019, 2022 and 2024, weighted. A birth is a woman reporting a birth in the past 12 months (FER); age is age at the survey, so some births happened a year younger. The ACS gives higher rates than birth registrations. Women still enrolled in school are left out, so the no-diploma group at young ages is a selected group. Rates by single year of age, as 3-year centred moving averages of births and women; graduates are shown from age 21. Education (SCHL): less than high school = no diploma; high school = diploma or GED; some college includes associate degrees.
```

```{note}
Nordic countries: Births: Eurostat [demo_faeduc](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/demo_faeduc/default/table), compiled from the national registers, by age at the birth; unknown education spread in proportion within age. Women by education and age: Statistics Finland [12bq](https://pxdata.stat.fi/PxWeb/pxweb/en/StatFin/StatFin__vkour/statfin_vkour_pxt_12bq.px/), Statistics Sweden [TAB4320](https://www.statistikdatabasen.scb.se/), Statistics Denmark [HFUDD11](https://www.statbank.dk/HFUDD11), Statistics Norway [08921](https://www.ssb.no/en/statbank/table/08921). Norway's population is published with a 30–39 band, so its rates stop there. All women are counted, including those still in education, so at 20–24 the lowest group includes women still in upper secondary school. Lower secondary or less = ISCED 0–2; tertiary = ISCED 5–8, including short-cycle degrees, Sweden's post-secondary under 3 years and Norway's tertiary vocational (fagskole). Years with a birth-coding break are dropped: Sweden before 2013, Norway before 2011, Finland after 2021. Denmark's births by education end in 2021.
```

```{note}
Other European countries: Births: Eurostat [demo_faeduc](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/demo_faeduc/default/table), from national birth registrations; unknown education spread in proportion within age. Women by education and age: Eurostat [lfsa_pgaed](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/lfsa_pgaed/default/table) (EU Labour Force Survey), 2008–2024, except Estonia. Births and women come from different sources that classify education separately, so a jump between years can be a classification change, and the LFS has a methodology break in 2021. LFS cells flagged as low reliability are dropped, which leaves gaps for the least educated in Croatia and Slovenia and for some young tertiary groups. Survey estimates for small groups, including the least educated in small countries, are noisy. Estonia: Statistics Estonia [RV142U](https://andmed.stat.ee/en/stat/RV142U) births and [RV0231U](https://andmed.stat.ee/en/stat/RV0231U) population by education, both from the register, 2017–2025; births at 40 and over are divided by women aged 40–44. Latvia's births by education start in 2017, Serbia's in 2010 and Turkey's in 2011; North Macedonia's end in 2021. Lower secondary or less = ISCED 0–2; upper secondary = ISCED 3–4; tertiary = ISCED 5–8.
```

```{note}
Mexico and Brazil: Mexico: births from INEGI [Estadística de Nacimientos Registrados](https://www.inegi.org.mx/programas/natalidad/) microdata, mother's age at the birth and schooling (ESCOL_MAD), births of each year registered in that year or the next (about 93% of all eventually registered); women from the [ENOE](https://www.inegi.org.mx/programas/enoe/15ymas/) third-quarter microdata, highest level with a grade passed. Primary or less = up to primary complete; secondary = secundaria, preparatoria, normal or carrera técnica; some university = at least one year of profesional. Brazil: births from DATASUS [SINASC](https://datasus.saude.gov.br/nascidos-vivos-desde-1994) by mother's residence, schooling ESCMAEAGR1 (first available in the 2014 file); women from IBGE [PNAD Contínua](https://www.ibge.gov.br/estatisticas/sociais/trabalho/17270-pnad-continua.html) third-quarter microdata, highest level attained (VD3004). Below secondary complete includes secondary incomplete; secondary complete includes tertiary incomplete. Births recorded as secondary with completion not stated (7–16%) are split between the two in proportion; other unknown schooling (1–2%) is spread in proportion within age. Both countries' rates mix registrations with a survey, so the education categories don't match exactly and levels across groups are less reliable than changes within a group. PNAD Contínua was run by telephone in 2020–21. At 20–24 education is unfinished for many.
```

```{note}
Colombia: births from DANE [Estadísticas Vitales](https://microdatos.dane.gov.co/index.php/catalog/878) birth microdata by year of birth, mother's age group and last level of study passed (NIV_EDUM); not stated (3–4%) spread in proportion within age. Women from the DANE [Gran Encuesta Integrada de Hogares](https://microdatos.dane.gov.co/index.php/catalog/819) (GEIH), July–September, highest level reached with a grade passed: the 2010–2020 "empalme" files reweighted to the 2018-census frame, and the redesigned survey from 2022. 2021 is left out because its public file is weighted to the 2005-census frame. 2020 was interviewed by telephone. Lower secondary or less = none, preschool, primary, basic secondary; upper secondary = media and normalista; tertiary = técnica profesional, tecnológica, university and postgraduate, with at least one year passed. Births are registrations and women a survey, so the education categories don't match exactly; levels across groups are less reliable than changes within a group.
```

```{note}
Taiwan: Ministry of the Interior household-register open data: births by mother's age and education, by registration year ([data.gov.tw 32945](https://data.gov.tw/dataset/32945)), over women in the register by age and education at the end of the year before and of the year itself ([32944](https://data.gov.tw/dataset/32944), [117988](https://data.gov.tw/dataset/117988)), 2016–2025. All births are counted, including those to mothers of foreign origin; mothers who are not yet citizens are not in the register, so rates are slightly overstated. Junior high or less = 國小畢業以下 and 國中畢業; senior high = 高中畢業, academic and vocational; junior college or more = 專科, university, master's and doctorate.
```
