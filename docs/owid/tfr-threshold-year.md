# TFR over time after crossing a threshold

The same threshold-based selection as
[TFR trajectories after crossing a threshold](tfr-threshold), but plotted
directly: x is year, y is TFR itself, with a dotted line marking the
threshold. Every major country (population over 1 million) whose TFR has
ever dropped below the chosen level is plotted from the year it first
crosses below through to its most recent year — even if it later recovers
back above the threshold, which does not cut the line short.

Drag the threshold slider to change which countries qualify; hover a point
to see its country and year.
