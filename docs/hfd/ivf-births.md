## Other countries

Few countries publish ART births by birth order. ART registries count treatment cycles by the woman's age at treatment; birth order is only available where the ART register is linked to the birth register.

### Denmark, France, Spain, UK and US

Each cell is MAR births in the row's group ÷ all live births in that group. For example, 12.1% of Danish first births were conceived with medically assisted reproduction (MAR). Birth order and age are separate groupings, not crossed.
